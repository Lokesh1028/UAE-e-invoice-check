Enter a UAE Tax Identification Number (TIN, 10 digits starting with 1 — not the 15-digit TRN). We check the live Peppol network registry (SML) and the Peppol Directory. If the TIN is not registered, an e-invoice cannot be delivered to that business today.

How this works, and what it is not

What we check

  • Each invoice row set is converted into the mandatory UAE format (PINT AE, UBL 2.1 XML) using only your data.
  • The XML is checked against the UBL 2.1 schema, the shared PINT rules and the UAE-specific rules published by the Ministry of Finance via OpenPeppol (version 1.0.2, 28 Jan 2026).
  • Every failed rule is translated into plain language with the field to look at and how to fix it.
  • Technical identifiers that your invoicing software will generate (UUID, specification IDs, line numbers) are filled in automatically; nothing else is assumed.

What we do not do

  • We do not store your file, your invoices or your customers. Processing happens in memory and the result lives only in your browser until you download the PDF.
  • We are not an Accredited Service Provider and do not transmit invoices. This is a readiness check, not a filing.
  • Rule sets change. Re-run the check when the Ministry publishes an update.
  • This is not tax advice. Confirm treatments with your tax agent.
